ROURI MARKET DOCS

Rouri pours liquidity into Uniswap v4 pools for coins on Robinhood Chain, straight from your wallet, the way pour.market does it

01 · overviewA second pool for any coin

Rouri lists stock tokens on Robinhood Chain and their ETH pools on Uniswap v4. You pour ETH and the coin into a pool as a full-range position and earn your share of every swap fee. The position is a Uniswap v4 NFT in your wallet; Rouri holds nothing.

03 · three signaturesApprove, allow, pour

├── approve     the coin to Permit2, once per coin
├── allow       Permit2 lets the Uniswap position manager spend it
├── pour        PositionManager.modifyLiquidities: mint position, settle pair, sweep leftover ETH back
├── new pool    the same transaction first initializes the pool at the market price
├── slippage    0.5%, 1% or 3% on both sides
├── deadline    15 minutes

04 · safetyWhy some pools are closed

A pool whose price is more than 25% away from the market price, or that holds no liquidity, is marked and Rouri will not pour into it. Pouring there would let the first arbitrageur buy your coins below market. Use the other tier, or wait until someone moves the price back.

05 · manageCollect and withdraw

├── uncollected fees    fee growth inside the range since your last update, read from StateView
├── collect             decrease liquidity by zero and take both tokens to your wallet
├── withdraw            burn the position and take everything, protected at 99% of the current amounts
├── your list           positions poured through Rouri are recorded by transaction; any other position can be added by id
