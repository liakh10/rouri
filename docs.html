Rouri into any coin. Earn the drip. How the estimate is calculated and what is simulated

01 · overviewA second pool for any coin

On Robinhood Chain a coin usually trades in one deep pool, and the fee from that pool belongs to whoever filled it. Rouri Market is a concept for opening a second pool on any coin that anyone can fill with both sides and earn a share of the swap fee from. On this site every pool, deposit and position is a simulation. The pool data it estimates from is real, and so is the $ROURI token.

02 · dataThe snapshot

The Pools table lists stock tokens trading on Robinhood Chain. For each one the site keeps the deepest pair found on DexScreener on 2026-09-13, stored in pools.js with the ETH price derived from a WETH pair. It does not update live.

03 · mathShare, fee, yearly

The calculator assumes trades split between the existing pool and your second pool in proportion to depth.

├── deposit        half in the coin at the snapshot price, half in USDG or ETH
├── share          your depth ÷ (pool depth + your depth)
├── fee per day    24h volume × tier × share
├── 30 days        fee per day × 30
├── yearly         fee per day × 365, also shown as a percent of the deposit
├── tiers          0.30% standard, 1.00% wild, the wild tier assumes trades are routed to it

Volume is prefilled from the snapshot and can be edited.

04 · impermanent lossPool versus holding

The price slider moves the coin from −90% to +300% against the quote asset and compares a full-range position with simply holding both sides.

├── r           1 + price move
├── loss        2√r ÷ (1 + r) − 1
├── examples    +100% → −5.72%, −50% → −5.72%, +300% → −20.0%

Fees earned are not subtracted from the loss in this view.

05 · simulatedFees that tick

Rouri it adds a position to this browser with the estimated fee per day. Uncollected fees grow every quarter of a second at that rate.

Collect
adds the uncollected amount to your collected total and restarts the clock
Withdraw
adds the uncollected amount and removes the position
Stored
localStorage keys rouri:positions and rouri:collected

09 · questionsQuestions

Can I add liquidity here?No. Nothing on this site is poured. The estimate uses real numbers so the simulation reads honestly.
Why would volume split by depth?Routers send trades where the price impact is lowest, and deeper pools usually give lower impact. It is an approximation.
Are the fees in Positions real?No. They are the estimate running on a clock.
What is real?$ROURI on Pons and the pool snapshot from DexScreener.
